# Lock screen

`Super + Escape` locks the screen. So does the Lock action of the [power wheel](../tools/roue.md), and [idle](hyprland.md#idle).

The time and the date stand on black. Type the password and press `Enter`. The field turns red on a wrong password, and yellow while Caps Lock is on. The battery level shows at the bottom, with `Wi-Fi` when it's connected.

Mute, mic mute and the playback keys work while the screen is locked.

Its look is in [`hyprlock.conf`](../../config/hyprland/hypr/hyprlock.conf): colours, sizes, positions, and the two texts of the field, `placeholder_text` and `fail_text`.
